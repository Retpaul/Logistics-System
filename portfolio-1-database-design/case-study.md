# Case study — EcoDispatch Logistics System

EcoDispatch is a realistic simulated delivery platform for owners, dispatchers, couriers and customers. The stored demonstration organisation remains **Demo Couriers**; EcoDispatch is the project name.

Customers book through QR links; staff create and assign orders; riders claim available jobs and update delivery status. Orders connect customers, optional riders/bikes/QR sources, status events, tracking samples and payment attempts. Tenants separate organisations; user accounts and refresh tokens support authentication. StoredFile records describe privately stored files.

The objectives are traceable deliveries, consistent assignments, confidential tenant records and efficient operational queries. Matching is manual assignment or claiming, not automated nearest-driver selection. Payment processing, deployed tracking and Portfolio 5 are not claimed as completed evidence.
